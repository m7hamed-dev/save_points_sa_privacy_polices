Privacy Policy

Habits - Professional Habit Tracking Application

Last Updated: November 22, 2024
Your Privacy Matters: Habits is designed with privacy at its core. All your data is stored locally on your device, and we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Habits ("we," "our," or "the app"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our professional habit tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally

Habits stores all data locally on your device using secure local storage (Hive database). This includes:

  • Habit Information: Your habit names, descriptions, goals, and tracking data
  • Progress Data: Your habit completion records, streaks, and statistics
  • User Preferences: App settings, theme preferences, and notification preferences
  • Calendar Data: Habit scheduling and completion dates
  • Charts and Analytics: Visual representations of your habit tracking progress

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we don't do:

  • We do NOT collect personal identification information
  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT access your contacts or photos (except when you explicitly choose to export data)
  • We do NOT send your data to external servers
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT share data with third parties

3. How We Use Your Information

All data processing happens locally on your device for the following purposes:

  • Habit Tracking: To record and display your habit completion data
  • Progress Visualization: To generate charts and statistics about your habits
  • Notifications: To send you local reminders for your habits (if enabled)
  • Background Processing: To update habit data and process notifications in the background, ensuring your app stays current even when not actively in use (iOS background fetch and processing modes). This feature can be enabled or disabled by you in your device's Settings at any time.
  • Data Export: To allow you to export your data in PDF or other formats when requested
  • Data Import: To allow you to import previously exported data (optional, user-initiated)
  • App Functionality: To provide core features like calendar views, streak tracking, and progress reports
  • Security: To protect your data with biometric authentication and screen lock features

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using Hive, a secure and efficient local database. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

4.2 Security Features

Habits includes the following security features:

  • Local Authentication: Optional biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition) or PIN/pattern lock to protect your data
  • Screen Lock: Additional screen lock protection for sensitive information
  • Encrypted Storage: Your data is stored securely on your device

4.3 Data Backup

Your data may be included in device backups (iCloud for iOS, Google Drive for Android) according to your device's backup settings. We recommend enabling device encryption for additional security.

5. Permissions

Habits may request the following permissions to provide its functionality. All permissions are optional and can be granted or denied by you. The app will function with reduced features if certain permissions are denied.

5.1 Notification Permissions

Purpose: To send you local reminders for your habits at custom times you set.

  • iOS: Notification permissions are requested when you enable notifications for a habit
  • Android: Notification permissions are requested when you enable notifications for a habit
  • Usage: Used only to deliver local notifications on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers
  • Control: You can enable or disable notifications for individual habits or globally in app settings

5.2 Biometric Authentication Permissions

Purpose: To secure your data with biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode).

  • iOS: Face ID or Touch ID permission (NSFaceIDUsageDescription) - requested only when you enable biometric lock in app settings
  • Android: Biometric authentication permission - requested only when you enable biometric lock
  • Usage: Used only for local authentication to unlock the app. Biometric data never leaves your device
  • Control: Completely optional. You can use the app without enabling biometric authentication
  • Library: local_auth (^2.1.0)

5.3 File System and Storage Permissions

Purpose: To save your habit data locally and access files for import/export functionality.

  • iOS: File system access for app's document directory (automatically granted, no user permission required)
  • Android: Storage permissions for saving and accessing files
  • Usage: Used to store habit data locally and access files for data import/export
  • Libraries: path_provider (^2.1.0), hive (^2.2.3), hive_flutter (^1.1.0), shared_preferences (^2.2.0)

5.4 File Picker Permissions

Purpose: To allow you to select files for data import (user-initiated only).

  • iOS: Document picker access - requested only when you choose to import data
  • Android: File access permission - requested only when you choose to import data
  • Usage: Used only when you explicitly choose to import previously exported data
  • Control: Only requested when you initiate an import action
  • Library: file_picker (^8.1.2)

5.5 Sharing and Export Permissions

Purpose: To allow you to export and share your data in PDF or other formats.

  • iOS: Share sheet access - requested only when you choose to export/share data
  • Android: Share intent permission - requested only when you choose to export/share data
  • Usage: Used only when you explicitly choose to export or share your habit data
  • Control: Only requested when you initiate an export/share action
  • Libraries: share_plus (^7.0.0), pdf (^3.11.1), printing (^5.13.3)

5.6 Background Processing Permissions (iOS)

Purpose: To allow the app to update habit data and process notifications in the background.

  • Background App Refresh: UIBackgroundModes - "fetch" mode
  • Background Processing: UIBackgroundModes - "processing" mode
  • BGTaskScheduler: Identifier: com.savepoints.sa.habits.refresh
  • Usage: Used to update habit data and process notifications when the app is not actively in use
  • Control: You can enable or disable Background App Refresh for Habits in iOS Settings:
    Settings → Habits → Background App Refresh
  • Important: When disabled, the app will only process data when actively in use. No data is transmitted to external servers during background operations

5.7 Network Connectivity (No Data Transmission)

Purpose: To check network connectivity status (for potential future features).

  • Usage: Only checks if device is connected to network. No data is transmitted
  • Library: connectivity_plus (^4.0.1)
  • Note: The app functions completely offline. Network checking is for potential future features only
Privacy Guarantee: All permissions are used solely for the stated purposes and do not involve data transmission to external servers. Background capabilities (iOS background fetch and processing modes) are used only to maintain app functionality and deliver notifications locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers during background operations.

User Control: You have full control over all permissions. You can:

  • Enable or disable notifications for individual habits or globally
  • Enable or disable biometric authentication
  • Control background processing through iOS Settings → Habits → Background App Refresh
  • Grant or deny file access when importing/exporting data
  • All permissions are optional - the app will function with reduced features if permissions are denied

6. Data Sharing and Export

6.1 User-Initiated Sharing

You have full control over your data. You can export and share your habit data using the app's export features. This is entirely optional and initiated by you.

6.2 No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties for marketing or any other purposes. Your data remains private and under your control.

7. Third-Party Services

Habits does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection for core functionality.

The app uses the following open-source libraries for functionality (all processing is done locally):

  • Hive & Hive Flutter: Local NoSQL database for storing habit data
  • Shared Preferences: Simple key-value storage for app settings
  • Syncfusion Flutter Charts: Data visualization and chart generation
  • Table Calendar: Calendar views for habit scheduling
  • Flutter Local Notifications: Local notification scheduling and delivery
  • Local Auth: Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition)
  • Flutter Screen Lock: Screen lock protection features
  • Path Provider: File system access for local storage
  • File Picker: File selection for data import/export
  • Share Plus: Native sharing functionality for exporting data
  • PDF & Printing: PDF generation and printing capabilities
  • Flutter Bloc: State management architecture
  • Get It: Dependency injection framework
  • Dio: HTTP client (used only for potential future features, currently offline-only)
  • Connectivity Plus: Network connectivity checking (no data transmission)

Important: All these libraries process data locally on your device. None of them transmit your data to external servers or third-party services. The app remains fully functional without an internet connection.

8. Children's Privacy

Habits does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since all data is stored locally and we don't collect any information, the app is safe for users of all ages.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

9.1 Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as you use the app. You have complete control over your data.

9.2 Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data and can delete it at any time using the following methods:

  • Delete Individual Habits: You can delete individual habits within the app at any time
  • Clear All Data: You can clear all your data (habits, progress, settings, and preferences) directly from within the app's settings. This action permanently removes all locally stored data and cannot be undone
  • Device Settings: You can clear all app data through your device settings (iOS: Settings → Habits → Delete App Data, or Android: Settings → Apps → Habits → Storage → Clear Data)
  • Uninstall the App: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all local data stored on your device

Important: When you clear all data, all your habits, progress tracking, statistics, settings, and preferences will be permanently deleted from your device. This action cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data before clearing if you want to keep a backup.

  • Modification: You can edit or update your habits and data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete any or all of your data at any time, including clearing all data through the app settings or device settings
  • Complete Data Removal: You can clear all your data (habits, progress, statistics, settings, and preferences) at any time, either through the app's settings or your device settings
  • Export: You can export your data in various formats (PDF, etc.) before clearing it
