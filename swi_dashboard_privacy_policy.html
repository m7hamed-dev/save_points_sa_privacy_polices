1. Introduction

Welcome to SWI Dashboard ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using SWI Dashboard, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Phone Number: Your mobile phone number for account identification and authentication
  • Password: Encrypted password for secure account access and authentication
  • Account Information: Username, email address, and authentication credentials
  • Profile Data: User preferences, settings, and customization options
  • Contact Information: Additional contact details for support or communication purposes

2.2 Location Data

Our application requires location services to identify and display the nearest refrigerators to your current position. We collect:

  • Real-Time Location: Your current GPS coordinates to find the nearest refrigerators
  • Precise Location: GPS coordinates for map-based features and navigation
  • Location History: Historical location data for operations tracking and route optimization
  • Geocoding Data: Address information derived from coordinates
Note: Location access is essential for finding nearby refrigerators. You can control location permissions through your device settings, but disabling location services will prevent the app from showing you the nearest refrigerators and may limit other functionality.

2.3 Device Information

We automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information
  • App version and configuration
  • Internet connection type and status
  • Language preferences (Arabic/English) for localization

2.4 Microphone Access

Our application may request microphone access for audio-related features. This permission is only used when you explicitly use features that require audio input.

Note: Microphone access is optional and only requested when needed for specific features. You can control this permission through your device settings.

2.5 Network Access

Our application requires network access to function properly:

  • Internet Connection: Required for API calls, data synchronization, and loading remote content
  • Local Network: May be used for debugging and development purposes
  • Network Monitoring: We check your internet connection status to provide appropriate functionality

2.6 Files and Media

When you use file-related features, we may access:

  • Images: Photos selected through the image picker for uploads or profile pictures. This includes access to your camera and photo library when you grant permission.
  • Documents: PDF files, CSV files, Excel files, and other documents you choose to share, view, or export
  • Screenshots: Captured screenshots when using the screenshot feature
  • File Picker: Files you explicitly select through the file picker feature
Important: We only access files that you explicitly select or authorize. We do not scan or access your device storage without permission. Camera and photo library access is only used when you choose to take or select photos within the app.

2.7 Usage Data

We collect information about how you interact with the app:

  • Features and pages accessed
  • Time spent in the application
  • Actions performed and operations completed
  • Error logs and crash reports
  • Performance metrics

7.2 Permissions Control

You can control app permissions through your device settings:

  • Location (iOS): Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Swi Dashboard
  • Location (Android): Settings > Apps > SWI Dashboard > Permissions > Location
  • Camera (iOS): Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > Swi Dashboard
  • Camera (Android): Settings > Apps > SWI Dashboard > Permissions > Camera
  • Photo Library (iOS): Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Swi Dashboard
  • Storage (Android): Settings > Apps > SWI Dashboard > Permissions > Storage
  • Microphone (iOS): Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > Swi Dashboard
  • Microphone (Android): Settings > Apps > SWI Dashboard > Permissions > Microphone
Note: Some features may not work properly if you revoke certain permissions. Location services are essential for finding nearby locations and displaying maps.
