Privacy Policy

Habits - Professional Habit Tracking Application

Last Updated: November 22, 2024
Your Privacy Matters: Habits is designed with privacy at its core. All your data is stored locally on your device, and we do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Habits ("we," "our," or "the app"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our professional habit tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally

Habits stores all data locally on your device using secure local storage (Hive database). This includes:

  • Habit Information: Your habit names, descriptions, goals, and tracking data
  • Progress Data: Your habit completion records, streaks, and statistics
  • User Preferences: App settings, theme preferences, and notification preferences
  • Calendar Data: Habit scheduling and completion dates
  • Charts and Analytics: Visual representations of your habit tracking progress

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we don't do:

  • We do NOT collect personal identification information
  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT access your contacts or photos (except when you explicitly choose to export data)
  • We do NOT send your data to external servers
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT share data with third parties

3. How We Use Your Information

All data processing happens locally on your device for the following purposes:

  • Habit Tracking: To record and display your habit completion data
  • Progress Visualization: To generate charts and statistics about your habits
  • Notifications: To send you local reminders for your habits (if enabled)
  • Data Export: To allow you to export your data in PDF or other formats when requested
  • App Functionality: To provide core features like calendar views, streak tracking, and progress reports

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using Hive, a secure and efficient local database. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

4.2 Security Features

Habits includes the following security features:

  • Local Authentication: Optional biometric authentication (fingerprint/face recognition) or PIN/pattern lock to protect your data
  • Screen Lock: Additional screen lock protection for sensitive information
  • Encrypted Storage: Your data is stored securely on your device

4.3 Data Backup

Your data may be included in device backups (iCloud for iOS, Google Drive for Android) according to your device's backup settings. We recommend enabling device encryption for additional security.

5. Permissions

Habits may request the following permissions to provide its functionality:

  • Notifications: To send you local reminders for your habits
  • Biometric/Local Authentication: To secure your data with fingerprint or face recognition
  • Storage: To save your habit data locally and export files
  • File Access: To allow you to export data as PDF or other formats

All permissions are used solely for the stated purposes and do not involve data transmission to external servers.

6. Data Sharing and Export

6.1 User-Initiated Sharing

You have full control over your data. You can export and share your habit data using the app's export features. This is entirely optional and initiated by you.

6.2 No Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with third parties for marketing or any other purposes. Your data remains private and under your control.

7. Third-Party Services

Habits does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection for core functionality.

The app uses the following open-source libraries for functionality (all processing is done locally):

  • Syncfusion Charts (for data visualization)
  • Table Calendar (for calendar views)
  • Hive (for local data storage)

8. Children's Privacy

Habits does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since all data is stored locally and we don't collect any information, the app is safe for users of all ages.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

9.1 Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as you use the app. You have complete control over your data.

9.2 Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual habits within the app
  • Clearing app data through your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app (this will permanently delete all local data)

10. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control and the following rights:

  • Access: You can access all your data within the app at any time
  • Modification: You can edit or update your habits and data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete any or all of your data at any time
  • Export: You can export your data in various formats (PDF, etc.)
  • Portability: Your exported data can be used with other applications

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. We will notify you of any significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Providing an in-app notification when you update to a new version

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

12. International Users

Since Habits stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit data to servers, there are no international data transfer concerns. Your data remains on your device regardless of your location.
