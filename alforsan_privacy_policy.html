At Alforsan, we are committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information obtained through the Alforsan dashboard for laundry management.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you use the Alforsan dashboard, we collect the following information:

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Technical Support: Provide assistance and resolve technical issues
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following cases:

3.1 With Service Providers

3.2 For Legal Purposes

  • In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. Data Protection and Security

We take strict security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: Use SSL/TLS encryption for all data transfers
  • Secure Storage: Store data in secure and protected servers
  • Authentication: Protect accounts with strong passwords
  • Monitoring: Continuous monitoring of suspicious activities
  • Security Updates: Regular updates to security systems
  • Limited Access: Restrict data access to authorized employees only
⚠️ Important Note: Despite taking all security measures, we cannot guarantee 100% data security over the internet. We recommend protecting your account information and not sharing it with others.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to access your personal information and know how it is used
  • Correction: The right to correct or update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: The right to request deletion of your personal information (in accordance with applicable laws)
  • Objection: The right to object to processing your information for certain purposes
  • Data Portability: The right to obtain a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: The right to withdraw your consent to data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. We may retain some information for longer periods for the following purposes:

  • Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Resolving disputes and enforcing our agreements
  • Preventing fraud and maintaining security

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable time period, unless retention is required by law.

7. Children's Privacy

The Alforsan dashboard is not directed at children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 18 years of age, we will take steps to delete this information as soon as possible.
